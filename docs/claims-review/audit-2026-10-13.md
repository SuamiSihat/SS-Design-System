## Needs a decision

1. **Physta® wording.** Ask Biotropics for the approved description and mark usage, then replace the neutral text on the Physta® card.
2. **Home page stat rail.** "124 Tests Passing" and "5 Sub-brands" are typed in by hand (`src/lib/components/home/HomeHero.svelte`). The suite now runs more than 124 checks. Either update the figures at each release or replace them with facts CI enforces (for example "0 accessibility violations").
3. **`static/content/vision-mission.md`.** Health-education copy (risks of unregulated stimulants; erectile dysfunction as an early sign of metabolic disease) and criticism of "aggressive commercial clinics" selling "RM5,000+ packages". Not a product claim, but worth a regulatory read, because the SS Clinic playbook moved competitor content to staff-only.
4. **Hard-coded catalogue cards.** Partner, legacy and bioactive cards in `src/routes/products/+page.svelte` are still written in Svelte, against Rule 11. Moving them to metadata files would bring them under `npm run lint` and the product schema.
