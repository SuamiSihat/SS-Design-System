# Rejal + Dr Mit Ring — Fix & Premium Update Plan

Date: 12 Oct 2026 (rev. 3: gold foil; Pantone + tokens in master spec) · Owner: SS Ecom (SSE) · Builds on `rejal-drmitring-guideline-plan.md`

> This is a design and compliance-risk plan, not legal advice. The final label text and ad copy should be checked by a Malaysian regulatory consultant, or confirmed directly with KKM's Food Safety and Quality Division (BKKM), NPRA (Food-Drug Interface classification) and the Medical Device Authority (MDA) before printing.

## 0. Decisions (12 Oct 2026)
1. **Dr Mit Ring: Path A, condom accessory.** Every erection, ejaculation, venous and "kekerasan" claim is removed from the box, leaflet, sachet, site, marketplace listings and ads.
2. **Bawang Lanang "450ml" is a 500ml jar.** The label must declare the **net content of what's inside, by weight (g)**, not the jar capacity. To do: weigh the filled product and set the declared net weight (e.g. "Berat bersih: ___ g"). The same applies to the 35ml (declare g). The brand list's "450ml / 500ml" are internal names only.
3. **Finish tier: open.** Recommendation in section 3.
4. **Dr Ismail Tambi is no longer associated.** Remove his name, likeness and "lineage" from all Dr Mit Ring assets. Use **general wording, with no individual doctor** (see 4b).

## 1. Guiding principles
1. **Premium means restraint.** Fewer claims, fewer badges, more craft: finish, typography, real photography.
2. **Food stays food.** Rejal is sold as food under the Food Act 1983 and Food Regulations 1985. Any wording that suggests treating or preventing a disease, or affecting sexual function, can push it into NPRA's traditional-product category (registration required) or make it non-compliant.
3. **The device stays a condom accessory, or gets registered.** Dr Mit Ring's medical-type claims (erection, ejaculation, venous leakage) describe a medical-device purpose, which falls under MDA (Act 737), not NPRA. Either remove them or register the device.
4. **Every seal must be ours and current.** Halal, MeSTI and Made in Malaysia marks may only appear when the certificate covers that product and that manufacturer.
5. **BM first** on all mandatory label text; English is optional and secondary.

## 3. Rejal — premium direction
**Positioning:** *Warisan Melayu, untuk lelaki yang melabur dalam kesihatan.* (Malay heritage, for men who invest in their health.) Heritage, dignity, craft. The tengkolok is a symbol of dignity, not a health claim.

**Finish recommendation (decision 3)**
- **All products:** matte black soft-touch stock + **gold foil emblem** (matched to PANTONE 871 C). At label scale, foil adds little unit cost and gives the whole range one premium signal.
- **Premium tier only** (Madu Tualang 1KG, Bawang Lanang 500ml jar, gift box): add **spot UV wordmark** + rigid gift box with debossed emblem.
- **Small labels** (35ml, Stevia 73×27mm): foil only, no spot UV; registration on tiny labels is unreliable.

**Design moves**
1. **Redraw the tengkolok as a vector line engraving** (single-colour, banknote style). This fixes the low-res raster (~226px in every file) and allows foil or emboss.
2. **Finish:** matte black soft-touch label stock; spot-UV "REJAL" wordmark (premium tier); **gold foil** on the emblem ring (matches the gold caps and quality-seal band); blue stays as the print colour on the ribbon (PANTONE 660 C). Heritage Gold is finish-only; no green (implies organic), no flat yellow.
3. **One emblem system:** a fixed lockup (Jawi arc + rings + ribbon) in two variants, QUALITY | ORIGINAL and QUALITY | NATURAL. Same size ratio on every SKU.
4. **Calm front panel:** only the wordmark, emblem, product name, size box and the Jawi tagline. Move all badges and certificates to the back or side panel.
5. **Typography:** one condensed display (wordmark/product names), Poppins for body text, wide-tracked caps for labels. Retire the older light condensed face.
6. **Packaging extras (premium tier):** numbered batch sticker, "QUALITY SEAL" shrink band kept, rigid gift box for honey 1kg + Bawang Lanang (black, debossed emblem).
7. **Photography:** real product on dark stone/wood, warm low-key light, raw ingredients (Tualang honeycomb, bawang lanang bulbs, stevia leaf). No AI-generated labels.

**Copy (food-safe examples)**
- Bawang Lanang + Madu Tualang: "Bawang lanang (bawang tunggal) diperam dalam madu Tualang asli. Resipi warisan, dibuat di Malaysia."
- Madu Tualang Asli: "Madu liar dari pokok Tualang, hutan Pahang. Dihasilkan oleh lebah *Apis dorsata*, spesies lebah madu terbesar." (Avoid "terbesar di dunia" as a bare claim.)
- Stevia: "Pemanis stevia original. 0 kalori setiap hidangan." (A nutrient content claim; confirm it meets the "free" criteria.)

**Label checklist (each SKU)**
Product name (BM) · ingredient list in descending order · net content (**g/kg for honey and Bawang Lanang**, ml for Stevia) · nutrition panel in kcal/kJ · manufacturer + distributor name/address · expiry date ("Tarikh luput / Guna sebelum") · batch no. · storage · halal/MeSTI only if valid for that manufacturer · infant warning (honey products) · preservative listed with its function (Stevia: "Pengawet (natrium benzoat)").

## 4. Dr Mit Ring — premium direction
**Positioning:** *A discreet, reusable condom accessory designed in Malaysia.* In this category, discretion is what reads as premium.

**4b. Credit line, replacing "Ciptaan Dr Ismail Tambi"**
- Recommended: "Direka oleh SuamiSihat · Malaysia" (Designed by SuamiSihat · Malaysia).
- Alternative: "Dibangunkan bersama SS Clinic" (Developed with SS Clinic), naming the clinic as an organisation, with no individual doctor and no "doctor recommended" language. Use only if SS Clinic actually took part in development, and check that the clinic's own advertising rules allow it.
- Not recommended: naming Dr Amir or Dr Rakesh. Doctors endorsing a product is restricted by MMC ethics guidance, and a doctor's face on a Path A accessory signals a medical purpose.
- The "Dr" in the brand name stays: DR MIT RING is a registered trademark (TM2023032015). Don't pair it with doctor photos, white coats or "doctor recommended" lines, so the name isn't read as a medical endorsement.
- The class 10 registration ("adult sexual aids / sexual activity devices") matches Path A positioning.

**Design moves**
1. **Discreet box:** matte off-white or black base, lime used only as the signal colour (ring, wordmark pill outline, one band). Drop the camouflage blobs.
2. **Replace the cartoon fist** with a clean line drawing of the ring (top orientation marker, groove), or a real macro photo of the lime ring.
3. **Keep the wordmark pill** ("dr mit ring", rounded lowercase) as the anchor; tighten clear space; one size per face.
4. **Badges:** keep "2 Rings" (restyled as a small lime chip, not a yellow coin) and "Asian Size". Remove unverified badges (R10).
5. **Leaflet:** clean bilingual layout with how-to line illustrations, care, reuse count (3–5), and "Hentikan penggunaan jika rasa sakit atau kebas" safety line.
6. **Ads:** black or deep-green type on off-white, real product photography, black pill CTA. No red x panic layout, no brush script, no yellow on lime.
7. **Colour fix:** text on lime is black (6.9:1); white text only on `#1A7A30` (5.4:1).

**Regulatory path (decision needed)**
- **Path A — Condom accessory (fast).** The product is described only as keeping a condom in place: fit, comfort, reuse, care. Remove every erection, ejaculation, venous or "kekerasan" claim from the box, leaflet, website and ads. This keeps it as clear of medical-device purpose as possible; still get an MDA classification opinion in writing.
- **Path B — Register with MDA (slower, unlocks claims).** If the business wants to say it supports erections, register it as a medical device under Act 737 (needs an authorised representative, conformity assessment, technical file). Claims are then limited to the registered intended use, and ads must follow MDA/KKM rules.

**Rules for "SuamiSihat®" as the official brand**
1. **Use ® with the registered logo artwork** (SS mark + SuamiSihat wordmark, in the registered colours): website header, ads, retail/marketplace stores, training/workshop materials, printed stationery and packaging paper. All of these fall under classes 16/35/41.
2. **Plain text:** write "SuamiSihat®" at the **first prominent mention** on a page, then "SuamiSihat". Because the word alone is disclaimed, the ® refers to the registered mark; never claim exclusive rights to the word itself ("only we may use the word SuamiSihat").
3. **Mono and other-colour logo variants** (dark, mono, reversed): the registration is colour-limited, so these aren't the exact registered form. Use them without ®, or use ™. Consider filing a black-and-white version (B&W filings cover all colours) to close this gap.
4. **On product labels** (Rejal food, Dr Mit Ring): "SuamiSihat" appears as the brand house / distributor ("Diedarkan oleh SuamiSihat Ecommerce Sdn Bhd"). The SS logo with ® is fine on the label as the house brand. Don't imply the registration covers the product itself, and don't put ® on "Rejal".
5. **Sub-brand logos** (SS Health, SS Clinic, SS Wellness, SS Ecom, SS Tech) are separate artworks: no ® unless they're registered; use ™ or nothing.
6. **Replace "SuamiSihat™"** across the design system with the rules above (the site currently uses ™ throughout).
7. **Recommended filings:** SS logo in B&W; SuamiSihat in class 3/5/10/29/30 if the house brand will appear on goods; REJAL composite mark in classes 29 + 30.
