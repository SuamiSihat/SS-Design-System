# Brand Voice & Editorial Standards

## Strategic Overview

SuamiSihat™ communicates with **clarity, empathy, and clinical authority**. Our verbal identity supports our core mission: empowering men across Southeast Asia to take proactive ownership of their health with confidence, scientific understanding, and dignity.

This document is the authoritative editorial standard for all SuamiSihat™ entities. For SS Clinic-specific copy strategy and the Brand Chorus doctrine, refer to: [SS Clinic Brand Positioning (SSC-STR-001)](https://assets.suamisihat.myds.me/doc/?doc=clinic-positioning)

---

## Core Voice Attributes

| Attribute | Definition | ✅ Preferred Expression | ❌ Prohibited Expression |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Empowering** | Fosters self-efficacy and proactive care rather than fear. | *"Taking charge of your vitality starts with small, daily steps."* | *"If you don't act now, you are failing your family."* |
| **Empathetic & Warm** | Approachable and respectful without informal slang. | *"Our clinical team is here to guide your wellness journey."* | *"Hey bro, check out this quick fix!"* |
| **Clinically Clear** | Explains medical concepts in plain, accessible language. | *"Blood pressure reflects how hard your heart works to circulate blood."* | *"Monitor your haemodynamic perfusion indices immediately."* |
| **Evidence-Grounded** | Rooted in peer-reviewed clinical research and approved protocols. | *"Clinical studies indicate positive outcomes when combined with physician guidance."* | *"100% guaranteed miracle cure in 24 hours."* |
| **Culturally Resonant** | Respectfully integrates natural Malaysian bilingual nuances. | Natural English paired thoughtfully with Bahasa Malaysia cultural touchpoints. | Unnatural clinical jargon or forced colloquialisms. |

## Tone by Platform & Context

### 1. Clinical & Educational Health Content
- Calm, reassuring, and objective.
- Grounded in medical evidence without alarmist framing.
- Encourages informed action and physician consultation.

### 2. Digital Applications & Telehealth
- Clear, concise, and action-oriented.
- Positive progress framing over guilt-inducing reminders.
- *Example:* *"Ready for your weekly health check-in?"* instead of *"You missed 5 consecutive check-ins."*

### 3. System Alerts & Notifications
- Respectful, informative, and transparent.
- Clearly states the issue and provides an immediate next action.
- *Example:* *"We encountered an issue updating your records. Please tap below to retry."*

### 4. Public Communications & Campaigns
- Uplifting, dignified, and community-centered.
- Elevates men's vital role in family leadership and well-being.
- Official Hashtags: `#SuamiSihat` `#KesihatanLelaki` `#ModernMasculineHealth`

## Language & Terminology Standards

### Bilingual Code-Switching (Bahasa Malaysia & English)
SuamiSihat™ serves a multilingual Malaysian demographic. Natural code-switching should feel seamless:
- Use Bahasa Malaysia for cultural resonance and emotional connection: *"Kesihatan anda adalah tiang kekuatan keluarga."*
- Use English for technical, pharmaceutical, or diagnostic terminology: *"Lipid profile, HbA1c, Doctor Consultation"*.

### Dignified & Destigmatizing Terminology

| Approved Term | Avoid | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| `SuamiSihat™` | `Suamisihat`, `SS` (in public copy) | Preserves official trademark equity and capitalization. |
| **Health journey / Vitality protocol** | Medical compliance / Regiment | Encourages partnership rather than passive submission. |
| **Wellness check-in** | Patient data submission | Humanizes digital tracking. |
| **Discreet consultation** | Secret treatment | Conveys dignity and privacy without illicit connotations. |
| **Doctor-supervised care** | Guaranteed cure | Complies with MOH/NPRA advertising standards. |
| **semulajadi** | *fitrah* (in ad/marketing copy) | *"Fitrah"* carries theological connotations misaligned with clinical messaging. Use *"semulajadi"* (natural) in all patient-facing copy. |

## SS Clinic — Additional Copy Standards (SSC-STR-001)

These rules apply specifically to SS Clinic (SuamiSihat Clinic Sdn. Bhd.) across all touchpoints: Meta Ads, Google Ads, organic social, WhatsApp, in-clinic signage, and the website.

### Absolutely Prohibited Keywords & Phrases

The following shame-trigger, taboo-exploitation, and urgency-panic phrases are **permanently blacklisted** across all SS Clinic copy:

| ❌ Prohibited | Category | Compliant Alternative |
|---|---|---|
| *"Mati pucuk"* | Shame / Taboo Shock | *"Sinyal semulajadi badan..."* or *"Penurunan tenaga lelaki"* |
| *"Lemah batin"* | Shame / Taboo Shock | *"Kesihatan intim lelaki"* or *"Vitaliti suami"* |
| *"Pancut awal"* | Shame / Taboo Shock | *"Stamina dan daya tahan"* |
| *"Keras dalam 1 sesi!"* | False Speed Claim | *"Rawatan terbukti secara klinikal"* |
| *"MATI PUCUK?! RAWAT SEGERA!"* | Shock Opener | Open with empathy: *"Ramai suami rasa macam tu..."* |
| *"Masih tak kuat lagi ke?"* | Shame Language | *"Ini peringatan semulajadi badan."* |
| *"Tinggal 2 slot!"* | False Urgency / Scarcity | Remove countdown urgency entirely |
| *"Cepat book!"* | High-Pressure Sales | *"Mulakan Pemeriksaan Anda Hari Ini"* |

### Typography & Punctuation Rules

| Rule | Standard | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Em-dashes (`—`)** | **Prohibited** in all ad copy, WhatsApp scripts, video captions, and landing pages. | Creates visual noise; inconsistent with brand's clean, editorial voice. Use commas, colons, or clean line breaks instead. |
| **Exclamation marks** | Use sparingly — maximum 1 per visual. | Reduces association with low-tier shock advertising. |
| **All-caps sentences** | Prohibited. | Associated with panic/shame hooks used by low-tier competitors. |

### The Brand Chorus Doctrine

The Brand Chorus is SuamiSihat's most powerful compounding brand asset. It requires disciplined, consistent repetition of the clinic's core verbal spine across all voices and all channels.

**Primary Spine (Subang Jaya — Standard Malay):**
> *"Kita check dulu. Baru kita rawat."*

**Primary Spine (Kota Bharu — Kelantanese Dialect):**
> *"Kito check dulu. Baru kito rawat."*

**Mandatory implementation rules:**
1. This phrase must appear in the **final 3 seconds** of every doctor video, Reel, and TikTok.
2. The **website hero section** must display: *"Check Dulu. Rawat Dengan Tepat. Tiada Janji Palsu."*
3. Every **WhatsApp automated reply** must include: *"Di sini kami check profil darah dulu sebelum rawat."*
4. The **Formaloo Quiz** opening screen must display: *"Ini bukan borang biasa. Ini pemeriksaan awal anda."*
5. Every **doctor's social bio** must end with: *"Check Dulu. Rawat Dengan Tepat. | @suamisihat"*

> Never abandon these phrases because they feel repetitive internally. The audience only begins noticing pattern after the 5th repetition. Brand Chorus minimum commitment: **90 days**.

### Regional Voice Variants

| Region | Dialect Standard | Approved Slogan | Forbidden |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Subang Jaya (SJ)** | Conversational, modern, educated urban Malay | *"Kita check dulu. Baru kita rawat."* | Kelantanese dialect, coarse street slang |
| **Kota Bharu (KB)** | Authentic Kelantanese dialect (*Kecek Kelate*) | *"Kito check dulu. Baru kito rawat. Dari Kelate, untuk Kelate."* | KL/urban slang, mixing dialects |
