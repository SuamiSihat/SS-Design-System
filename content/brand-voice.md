# Brand Voice & Editorial Standards

## Strategic Overview

SuamiSihat™ communicates with **clarity, empathy, and clinical authority**. Our verbal identity supports our core mission: empowering men across Southeast Asia to take proactive ownership of their health with confidence, scientific understanding, and dignity.

---

## Core Voice Attributes

| Attribute | Definition | ✅ Preferred Expression | ❌ Prohibited Expression |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Empowering** | Fosters self-efficacy and proactive care rather than fear. | *"Taking charge of your vitality starts with small, daily steps."* | *"If you don't act now, you are failing your family."* |
| **Empathetic & Warm** | Approachable and respectful without informal slang. | *"Our clinical team is here to guide your wellness journey."* | *"Hey bro, check out this quick fix!"* |
| **Clinically Clear** | Explains medical concepts in plain, accessible language. | *"Blood pressure reflects how hard your heart works to circulate blood."* | *"Monitor your haemodynamic perfusion indices immediately."* |
| **Evidence-Grounded** | Rooted in peer-reviewed clinical research and approved protocols. | *"Clinical studies indicate positive outcomes when combined with physician guidance."* | *"100% guaranteed miracle cure in 24 hours."* |
| **Culturally Resonant** | Respectfully integrates natural Malaysian bilingual nuances. | Natural English paired thoughtfully with Bahasa Malaysia cultural touchpoints. | Unnatural clinical jargon or forced colloquialisms. |

---

## Tone by Platform & Context

### 1. Clinical & Educational Health Content
- Calm, reassuring, and objective.
- Grounded in medical evidence without alarmist framing.
- Encourages informed action and physician consultation.

### 2. Digital Applications & Telehealth
- Clear, concise, and action-oriented.
- Positive progress framing over guilt-inducing reminders.
- *Example:* *"Ready for your weekly health check-in?"* instead of *"You missed 5 consecutive check-ins."*

### 3. System Alerts & Notifications
- Respectful, informative, and transparent.
- Clearly states the issue and provides an immediate next action.
- *Example:* *"We encountered an issue updating your records. Please tap below to retry."*

### 4. Public Communications & Campaigns
- Uplifting, dignified, and community-centered.
- Elevates men's vital role in family leadership and well-being.
- Official Hashtags: `#SuamiSihat` `#KesihatanLelaki` `#ModernMasculineHealth`

## Language & Terminology Standards

### Bilingual Code-Switching (Bahasa Malaysia & English)
SuamiSihat™ serves a multilingual Malaysian demographic. Natural code-switching should feel seamless:
- Use Bahasa Malaysia for cultural resonance and emotional connection: *"Kesihatan anda adalah tiang kekuatan keluarga."*
- Use English for technical, pharmaceutical, or diagnostic terminology: *"Lipid profile, HbA1c, Doctor Consultation"*.

### Dignified & Destigmatizing Terminology

| Approved Term | Avoid | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| `SuamiSihat™` | `Suamisihat`, `SS` (in public copy) | Preserves official trademark equity and capitalization. |
| **Health journey / Vitality protocol** | Medical compliance / Regiment | Encourages partnership rather than passive submission. |
| **Wellness check-in** | Patient data submission | Humanizes digital tracking. |
| **Discreet consultation** | Secret treatment | Conveys dignity and privacy without illicit connotations. |
| **Doctor-supervised care** | Guaranteed cure | Complies with MOH/NPRA advertising standards. |
