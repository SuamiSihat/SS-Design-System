1X Clear Space & Exclusion Zone Isolation Standard
Maintain an unobstructed exclusion margin around all four perimeters of the wordmark equal to the height of the lowercase character x. No typography or graphical elements may encroach within this boundary.

Please Do
  • Use official artwork supplied by SuamiSihat™ without modification.
  • Select the correct light, dark or monochrome logo version for background contrast.
  • Maintain the illustrated x-based clear space around all sides.
  • Scale logos proportionally — preserve symbol and wordmark proportions.
  • Place logos on clean, uncluttered backgrounds with high contrast ratio.
Please Don't
  • Stretch, compress, rotate, skew or crop the logo.
  • Recolour, swap palette hues or rearrange the wordmark elements.
  • Add drop shadows, outlines, gradients, glows, or 3D bevel effects.
  • Place the logo on visually noisy, busy or low-contrast backgrounds.
  • Imply unapproved endorsement, certification, or false medical claims.

08 · Governance & Ecosystem Delivery

Client Representation & Dignity Standards

How internal staff, developers, and designers must represent the SuamiSihat™ brand across patient-facing portals, teleconsultation interfaces, clinics, and packaging.

60:30:10 Visual Hierarchy

Every client-facing screen must maintain a clean visual mass distribution: 60% Foundation (clinical canvas), 30% Secondary (structure & cards), and 10% Accent (reserved for the primary booking CTA).

  • 60% Canvas: #F8FAFC / #FFFFFF (Light) or #090D16 (Dark)
  • 30% Structure: #022057 (Prussian Blue) & #043388 (SS Blue)
  • 10% Focal CTA: #21A1F7 (Azure) or #FCE53D (Banana Yellow)

Dignity & Claims Integrity

Our positioning is Modern Masculine Healthcare. We speak with clinical precision, empathy, and dignity.

  • MASCULINE ≠ MACHO: Zero emasculation shame, bro-science myths, or fake urgency.
  • MOH / NPRA Truth: Zero 100% cure guarantees or unscientific claims.
  • Strict Text Neutrality: Text strictly uses #1C1C1C. Pure #000000 is forbidden for text.
