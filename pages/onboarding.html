Welcome to SuamiSihat™

A note to get you started

We are Southeast Asia's fastest-growing men's health brand, built on the belief that every man deserves clear, dignified, and accessible healthcare. You're now part of that mission. This guide covers your brand orientation, daily tools, and a first-week checklist.

If you have questions not answered here, your line manager or hr@suamisihat.com is your first stop.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted men's health brand in Southeast Asia.

Our Mission

Empower every touchpoint with a design language that is consistent, accessible, and purposefully built.

Our Values

Clinical precision, human-first design, and the courage to redefine what healthcare looks like.

Brand & Identity

How we look and sound

Our brand centres on SS Prussian Blue (#022057) and SS Blue (#043388), accented by Azure (#21A1F7). Warm gold tones (Lion #BD9A73, Arylide #E5D15C) are used for premium accents. Never use off-brand colours in external materials.

First Week Checklist

Click each item to mark it done. Progress is saved in your browser.

Day 1

  • Receive and set up your work laptop / device
  • Set up your @suamisihat.com work email
  • Generate your mail signature using the Signature Generator
  • Log into the SSNAS Dashboard and bookmark it
  • Complete HR paperwork and emergency contact form

Days 2-3

  • Read through the Brand System - especially Logo Rules and Colour Tokens
  • Review the Brand Guidelines PDF booklets
  • Download SS CAM and tools relevant to your role
  • Meet your team and schedule a 1-to-1 with your manager

Week 1

  • Understand your KPIs and your department OKRs
  • Familiarise yourself with our products (Product Catalogue)
  • Complete the data privacy and confidentiality briefing
  • Join relevant team channels (WhatsApp / email groups)
  • Share your first-week reflections with your manager

Your checklist is automatically saved in your browser. Come back anytime to pick up where you left off.
