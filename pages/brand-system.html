Icons & Logo Usage

Clear signals, every time. Our icons are precise, purposeful, and always on-brand. Here's how to use them correctly — and what to avoid.

Light Dark Click to toggle
How to Use Icons
Guidelines for icon usage in all SS products
  • Maintain adequate spacing — at least equal to the icon's own width on all sides
  • Use the appropriate colour variant for background (light or dark) for contrast compliance
  • Ensure icons are clearly visible and not competing with other visual elements
  • Scale icons proportionally — never stretch or distort beyond the original aspect ratio
Brand Integrity — Official Guidelines

Official, unmodified master-brand, parent-company and subsidiary logo kits may be downloaded for factual identification, editorial coverage, approved listings and standard partner references.

Please Do
  • Use official artwork supplied by SuamiSihat™ without modification
  • Select the correct light, dark or monochrome logo version for background contrast
  • Maintain the illustrated x-based clear space around all sides
  • Scale logos proportionally — preserve symbol and wordmark proportions
  • Place logos on clean, uncluttered backgrounds with high contrast ratio
  • Request formal approval for commercial, promotional or co-branded use
Please Don't
  • Stretch, compress, rotate, skew or crop the logo
  • Recolour, swap palette hues or rearrange the wordmark elements
  • Add drop shadows, outlines, gradients, glows, or 3D bevel effects
  • Place the logo on visually noisy, busy or low-contrast backgrounds
  • Imply unapproved endorsement, certification, or false medical claims
Safe Zone & Clear Space

The SuamiSihat™ logo uses fixed relationships between the symbol and wordmark. Maintain the illustrated x-based clear space around every side. Keep this protected area free from text, imagery, borders, partner logos and interface elements.
