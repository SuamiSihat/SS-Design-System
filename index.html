How to Use Icons

Our icons are designed to be simple and recognizable. When using icons:

  • Maintain adequate spacing around the icon (at least equal to the icon's width)
  • Use the appropriate color variant for your background (light or dark)
  • Ensure icons are clearly visible and not competing with other visual elements
  • Scale icons proportionally - never stretch or distort

Logo Usage

The SS Health logo is more than just a mark - it is a symbol of engineered trust, precision, and professionalism. Its consistent application is critical to upholding the credibility and strength of our brand. Using the logo correctly ensures visual clarity, brand recognition, and alignment with our identity as a performance-driven health brand.

To preserve its integrity, the logo must always be placed on clean, uncluttered backgrounds that support strong contrast and visibility. Avoid placing it on visually noisy, overly complex, or distracting images that could interfere with legibility or diminish the logo's impact.

Safe zone guide

Under no circumstance should the logo be modified, distorted, or altered in any way. This includes changing its proportions, orientation, colour, or composition, as well as adding effects such as shadows, gradients, strokes, or fades. The logo must only appear in its approved formats, as provided in this guideline.

Every aspect of the SS Health logo has been deliberately designed to express our identity with clarity and confidence. Proper usage across all platforms—digital, print, spatial, and clinical - ensures it remains a powerful and unmistakable symbol of who we are and what we stand for.
