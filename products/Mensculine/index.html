📝 1. Product Description

Doctor-formulated daily dermatological cream and gentle wash designed to maintain penile tissue elasticity, optimize microvascular blood flow, and ensure intimate skin hygiene worldwide.

Whitelabel Relation: Whitelabel counterpart of MENSS : FRÉSH Wash for international distribution.
📐 2. Spec, Size & Volume
  • • Net Weight: 50 g / 1.76 oz
  • • Packaging Format: Aluminum Matte Squeeze Tube with screw cap
  • • Tube Dimensions: 35 mm (Dia) × 125 mm (H)
  • • Outer Box: 40 mm × 40 mm × 130 mm (Bronze Foil Stamped)
🧪 3. Active Ingredients
  • Physta® Standardized Tongkat Ali Extract
  • L-Arginine Hydrochloride
  • Ascorbic Acid (Vitamin C)
  • Natural Emollient Base
📖 4. Directions for Use
  1. Apply pea-sized amount onto clean palm.
  2. Massage gently onto penile skin.
  3. Allow absorption; rinse lightly if used as wash.
  4. Apply twice daily after bathing.
🛑 5. Safety & Warnings
  • For external intimate application only.
  • Do not apply on broken or infected skin.
  • Store below 30°C in a dry place.
