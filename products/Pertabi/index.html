4. Community Initiatives & National Advocacy

Core national programmes organized by PERTABI (Persatuan Taat Bersama Isteri Malaysia · ROS: PPM-023-10-15122021) to champion family harmony and responsible fatherhood.

PROGRAMME 1 🏹
Archery & Sports Vitality

Traditional sunnah archery clinics and sports events building mental focus, physical posture, and core stamina among husbands.

PROGRAMME 2 📚
Husband Leadership Forums

Workshops and marital masterclasses educating men on emotional intelligence, intimacy health, and dignified family governance.

PROGRAMME 3 🤝
Grassroots Family Welfare

Community outreach providing counseling, healthcare access, and support for young couples and growing households.
