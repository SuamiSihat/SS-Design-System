# Our companies

SuamiSihat® is one brand used by five companies. This page says what each company does, how it should sound, which colour leads, and where to get its logo.

| Company | What it does | Leading colour |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SuamiSihat Holding (SS Health)** | The parent company. Owns the brand | Prussian Blue `#022057` |
| **SuamiSihat Clinic (SS Clinic)** | Clinics and telehealth | Prussian Navy `#022057` |
| **SuamiSihat Wellness (SS Wellness)** | Supplements and daily care | Azure `#21A1F7` |
| **SuamiSihat Ecommerce (SS Ecommerce)** | Online shop and delivery | Lion `#BD9A73` |
| **SuamiSihat Technology (SS Technology)** | Apps, websites and systems | Malibu `#6DC6EC` |

Not sure which logo to use? For anything the public sees, such as products, posters, social posts and screens, use the **SuamiSihat®** master brand. Use a company logo only when the work is about that company.

## SuamiSihat Holding (SS Health)

**SuamiSihat Holding Sdn. Bhd.** is the parent company and owns the SuamiSihat® brand. It uses the SS Health logo.

**Use it for:**
- Company, legal, investor and governance papers
- Group employment and finance documents
- Partner agreements and formal clinical governance

**Leading colour:** Prussian Blue `#022057`, for the logo, legal papers and executive stationery.

## SuamiSihat Clinic (SS Clinic)

**SuamiSihat Clinic Sdn. Bhd.** looks after patients directly: health checks, telehealth and personal care plans. It runs clinics in Subang Jaya and Kota Bharu.

> The full SS Clinic guide, with logos, colours, brand promise and downloads, is on the [SS Clinic guideline page](/products/ssclinic/).

**How it sounds:** reassuring, expert and warm, like a trusted older brother. Respectful, never shaming.

**What it stands for:** *"The Husband's Health Authority."* The clinic always checks first, and only then recommends treatment.
- **Core line:** *"Kami Tak Jual Rawatan. Kami Cari Punca."*
- **Three-part promise:** *"Check Dulu. Rawat Dengan Tepat. Tiada Janji Palsu."*

**The two clinics**

| Clinic | Situation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Subang Jaya** | Many clinics compete here, so we stand out by how we work |
| **Kota Bharu** | Few others offer this, so we are building the category |

| **30%** | Clinical White and Clinical Navy | `#FFFFFF` and `#0F4C75` | Cards and content panels |
| **10%** | Warm Clinic Gold | `#F4A261` | Buttons, badges and ratings |
