# Brand Voice & Tone

## Overview

SuamiSihat™ speaks with clarity, warmth, and authority. Our voice reflects the mission — empowering Malaysian husbands to take charge of their health with confidence and dignity.

---

## Core Voice Attributes

| Attribute | Description | Do | Don't |
|-----------|-------------|-----|-------|
| **Empowering** | We build confidence, not dependency | "You've got this." | "You need to…" |
| **Warm** | Friendly but not casual | "We're here with you." | "Hey bro!" |
| **Clear** | Plain language, no jargon | "Check your blood pressure." | "Monitor your haemodynamic indices." |
| **Honest** | Grounded in evidence | "Studies suggest…" | "Guaranteed to…" |
| **Culturally resonant** | Speaks to Malaysian men | Malay-English blend where appropriate | Pure clinical English only |

---

## Tone by Context

### Health Content
- Calm and reassuring
- Evidence-based, not alarmist
- Encouraging action without inducing anxiety

### App Notifications
- Brief and action-oriented
- Positive framing over negative
- Example: *"Time for your weekly check-in!"* not *"You haven't logged in 7 days."*

### Error Messages
- Apologetic but not dramatic
- Offer a clear next step
- Example: *"Something went wrong on our end. Let's try that again."*

### Social Media
- Conversational, warm, community-driven
- Light humour where appropriate
- Hashtags: `#SuamiSihat` `#KesihatanLelaki` `#JagaDiriLelaki`

---

## Language Guidelines

### Malay–English Code-Switching
SuamiSihat™ serves a Malaysian audience. Thoughtful blending of Bahasa Malaysia and English reflects our users' natural speech patterns.

**Preferred approach:**
- Use Malay for emotional/cultural resonance: *"Jaga diri, wahai suami."*
- Use English for clinical or technical terms: *"BMI, blood pressure, cholesterol"*
- Never mix mid-sentence unless it feels completely natural in context

### Inclusive Language
- Address the user as "you" — avoid gendered alternatives where possible
- Husband-focused but never excluding carers, partners, or family
- Avoid stigmatising language around mental health

## Writing Examples

### ✅ On-brand
