# Husband Clinic — Nationwide Men's Health Clinic Network

Husband Clinic is the premier clinical network partner of SuamiSihat™ Clinic (`02-SSC`). Operating specialized physical medical centres across Kuala Lumpur, Selangor, Johor Bahru, and Penang.

---

## 1. Clinical Services & Diagnostics

- **Comprehensive Andrology Profiling**: Total & Free Testosterone, SHBG, Prolactin, Estradiol, PSA, and Lipid profiles with 4-hour turnaround.
- **Penile Doppler Ultrasound**: Real-time hemodynamic evaluation of cavernosal arterial peak systolic velocity (PSV) and end-diastolic velocity (EDV).
- **Extracorporeal Shockwave Therapy (Li-ESWT)**: Non-invasive low-intensity acoustic shockwave therapy for penile angiogenetic revascularization.
- **Doctor Teleconsultations**: Secure, encrypted virtual video consultations with registered medical practitioners.
