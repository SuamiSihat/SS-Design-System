# PERTABI — Persatuan Taat Bersama Isteri Malaysia

PERTABI is the official community and NGO partner network supported by SuamiSihat™ Holding. Dedicated to promoting marital harmony, responsible fatherhood, psychological support, and holistic family health education throughout Malaysia.

---

## 1. Core Mission & Programs

- **Marriage Enrichment Workshops**: Evidence-based intimacy workshops and communication counseling for couples.
- **Men's Mental Health Circles**: Confidential peer-to-peer discussions addressing anxiety, paternal burnout, and mid-life transitions.
- **Community Wellness Screenings**: Free blood pressure, BMI, and glucose checkups conducted in conjunction with SuamiSihat™ Clinic outreach programs.
