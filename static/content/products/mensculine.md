Mensculine™ is the international flagship wellness and dermocosmetic brand by SuamiSihat™ (`05-SSE · International`). ## 1. Formulation Profile: Mensculine Care Cream (50g)

- **Bioactive Compound**: Physta® Standardized Eurycoma Longifolia (Tongkat Ali) aqueous extract (US Patent 7,132,117).
- **Nitric Oxide Precursors**: L-Arginine & L-Citrulline micro-encapsulated for rapid epidermal absorption.
- **Cooling & Sensation Agents**: Menthol, Butea Superba extract, and Vitamin E Acetate.
- **Vehicle Base**: Non-greasy vanishing oil-in-water (O/W) emulsion base that leaves zero residue on skin or fabric.
