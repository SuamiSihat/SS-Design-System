**Persatuan Taat Bersama Isteri Malaysia (PERTABI)** is an official national-level non-governmental society registered under Section 7 of the Societies Act 1966 (*PPM-023-10-15122021*). 

Operating under the motto **"Taat Setia Progresif"**, PERTABI redefines the modern husband’s role from passive stereotype to active, accountable, and healthy family leadership.

### Core Objectives & Pillars
* **Husband Education & Awareness**: Elevating awareness and skills in male health, spousal emotional communication, financial resilience, and parenting.
* **Family Research & Data Collection**: Systematic nationwide surveying to identify real-world pressures on Malaysian households and build evidence-grounded community solutions.
* **Marital Harmony & Dignity**: Platforming husbands to uphold, honor, and cherish women generally, and wives specifically.
* **Active Lifestyle & Community**: Mobilizing members through sports, charity runs, and official merchandise including the signature PERTABI Active Jersey.
