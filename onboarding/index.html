Staff Onboarding & Guidelines

Welcome to the SuamiSihat™ team. Follow this onboarding checklist to get your digital tools, design assets, and communications set up.

New Joiner Checklist

All Staff
Read and understand the 60:30:10 Visual Hierarchy and Neutral Black (#1C1C1C) rule.
All Staff
Generate your official corporate email signature using the Signature Tool.
Designers
Download the official Vector Marks Kit (.svg / .png) from the Downloads repository.
Developers
Import /assets/css/fluent.css and /assets/css/ss_theme.css into your app shell.
Clinic Team
Install SS-CAM desktop suite for Windows clinical camera integration.
